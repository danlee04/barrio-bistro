# Site photos

Drop the shop's photos here, with exactly these names. They are served straight
from `/images/...`, so replacing a file is enough — no build, no deploy step.

| File           | Where it shows                                    | Best shape                    | Aim for      |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------ |
| `hero.jpg`     | Behind the headline on the home page              | Landscape, 1600×1000 or wider | Under 400 KB |
| `story.jpg`    | The big photo beside "Our story", and on `/about` | **Portrait**, 1000×1250       | Under 300 KB |
| `kitchen.jpg`  | Small photo on the lower left of that pile        | **Square**, 800×800           | Under 200 KB |
| `counter.jpg`  | Small photo on the upper right of that pile       | **Square**, 800×800           | Under 200 KB |
| `interior.jpg` | The big photo beside "Visit" on the home page     | **Portrait**, 1000×1250       | Under 300 KB |
| `street.jpg`   | Small photo on the upper left of that pile        | **Square**, 800×800           | Under 200 KB |
| `table.jpg`    | Small photo on the lower right of that pile       | **Square**, 800×800           | Under 200 KB |

Notes:

- **JPG or WebP.** WebP is smaller at the same quality; either works.
- Only `hero.jpg` is **wide**. `story.jpg` and `interior.jpg` are **tall**, and each
  carries two small squares laid across its corners.
- The two piles are mirrored: the story's small photos sit lower-left and upper-right,
  the visit's sit upper-left and lower-right.
- Faces and the dish should sit near the middle — the edges get cropped on a phone.
- Until a file is here, the page shows a plain coloured panel in its place. Nothing breaks.
- Gallery photos do **not** go here. Those are uploaded in the admin, so the shop can
  change them without touching the code.
